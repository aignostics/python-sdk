---
itemId: SWR-APPLICATION-2-9
itemTitle: Monitor Application Processing Until Completion
itemHasParent: SHR-APPLICATION-2
itemType: Requirement
Requirement type: FUNCTIONAL
Layer: System (backend logic)
---

System shall monitor submitted application runs and shall wait for processing completion before proceeding to result retrieval. The system shall poll run status at regular intervals and shall detect when runs reach completed status.
